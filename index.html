About

Nenzy Brown is a self-taught founder based in Dubai, UAE, with 5+ years of hands-on experience in AI and blockchain. He is the founder of four ventures: Cryptohost USD (CUSD), a multichain stablecoin; Turbo USD, a DeFi protocol; PlateWallet, a payment network built into the vehicle license plate, launching in the UAE in 2026; and ProofReceipt, a live tool that gives freelancers and AI agents verifiable delivery receipts.

He holds no degree — his record is the work itself, including a paid escrow delivery verified through a platform dispute process in October 2026. He writes on dev.to and Hashnode and builds in public as @NenzyBrown. His focus: build useful financial infrastructure, and prove every claim with shipped work.
